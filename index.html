Our Story

A little cafe with a big, warm kitchen

Gloria's Cafe is a cozy, family-run breakfast spot tucked off Dickerson Rd, near the Truckee River. We cook American diner classics side by side with the Mexican comfort food we grew up on, so eggs benedict and chilaquiles both belong on the same table.

Everything starts from scratch: our ranchero sauce, our homemade beans, our hollandaise. Come as you are, grab a seat inside or out on the patio, and let us take care of the rest.

  • 🍳 Scratch-made sauces & beans
  • ☀️ Shaded outdoor patio
  • 🚗 Easy parking & curbside pickup
  • ♿ Wheelchair-accessible entrance
